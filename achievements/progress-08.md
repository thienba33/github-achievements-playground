# Tien trinh Open Sourcerer (8/8)

Huy hieu **Open Sourcerer** danh cho nhung dong gop cho cac du an ma nguon mo.

- Bronze: 10 dong gop cho du an ma nguon mo.
- Silver: 50 dong gop cho du an ma nguon mo.
- Gold: 100 dong gop cho du an ma nguon mo.
- Platinum: 1000 dong gop cho du an ma nguon mo.

Dong gop bao gom commit, pull request duoc chap nhan, bao cao loi va nhan xet co gia tri cho cac du an ma nguon mo tren GitHub.

File rieng cho Open Sourcerer, khong lien quan den BTG.
